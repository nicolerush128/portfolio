Cura Insurance: Brand Identity & Website Redesign

No logo, no colors, no visual language. Just a name.

CLIENTCura Insurance
ROLEBrand & Web Designer
SERVICESBrand Identity, Web Design, Creative Direction
YEARBrand 2022 · Site 2026

OVERVIEW

Cura Insurance came in with nothing: no logo, no colors, no visual language. Just a name. I built the brand foundation first, launched it across every touchpoint, and four years later redesigned the website on top of it.

01

Building the foundation

Logo, mark, color palette, and typography came first, before any digital product. Deep navy and sky blue keep it professional and calm.

The test for every decision: does this feel like someone you’d call when something goes wrong?

02

Everywhere the brand lives

Business cards, email signatures, mailers, social graphics, and ads. All built from one system, so Cura holds together from business card to website.

03

The website redesign

The new site restructures the homepage around clearer product navigation, stronger independent-agency positioning, and a streamlined quote process, with a two-column community hero, a six-product grid, Google-review social proof, and an organized navy footer.

04

Built to convert

The redesign builds on the original foundation, taking a brand that was built right and building a digital experience that converts.
