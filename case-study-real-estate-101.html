Real Estate 101: The Comic Book

Making real estate make sense to a room full of kids.

CLIENTThe Alms Group × Career to Classroom
ROLEDesigner & Illustrator
SERVICESEducation Design, Illustration, Brand Storytelling
YEAR2022–2025

OVERVIEW

The Alms Group joined the city’s Career to Classroom program to teach 4th graders about real estate. Instead of a handout, I proposed a comic book, meeting kids where they already are, in a medium they love.

4thgrade classrooms across the program
Every yearreprinted and taught again
100%original characters, story, and illustration
01

The idea

Real estate is an unfamiliar, abstract topic for kids. A comic uses visual storytelling to make it concrete (homes, buildings, land, buying, and selling) in a format they already love.

02

Building the story

I created original characters, built the narrative structure, and calibrated the vocabulary and visual complexity for 4th graders. Every panel had to work for students and for the teachers in the room.

03

In the classroom

After the first live classroom presentation, the comic clicked with students right away, and the client loved how it represented them in the community.

It’s now reprinted and taught in 4th-grade classrooms every year.
