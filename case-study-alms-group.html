The Alms Group: Brand Collateral Redesign

Two audiences, two brochures, one cohesive organization.

CLIENTThe Alms Group
ROLEDirector of Marketing & Philanthropy
SERVICESBrand Identity, Print Design, Creative Direction
YEAR2022–2025

OVERVIEW

The Alms Group’s print materials looked dated and didn’t hold together, which undercut conversations with donors and clients. The job: two distinct pieces for two distinct audiences, without losing one cohesive organization.

2brochures for two audiences: donors and clients
12local businesses carrying them as leave-behinds
EveryKansas State University department stocked
1unified visual foundation behind it all
01

Foundation first

Before any layout, I set a unified visual foundation so both pieces would read as one organization.

02

Two audiences, two messages

One brochure leads with mission for donors; the other leads with credibility for prospective clients. Cohesion without uniformity.

03

Out in the world

The pieces became go-to conversation starters, placed at a dozen local businesses serving people new to the area and in every department at Kansas State University.

They helped turn introductions into leads.
