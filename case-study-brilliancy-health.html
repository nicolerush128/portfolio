Brilliancy Health: Brand, Product & Event Launch

A new health-tech division. No brand, no site, no event. Just months to launch all three.

CLIENTBrilliancy Quantum
ROLECreative Director
SERVICESBrand Identity, Web Design, Creative Direction
YEAR2026

OVERVIEW

When Brilliancy Quantum introduced Brilliancy Health, a new division with five health-tech product lines, I built the brand identity from the ground up and carried it through the website and the division’s launch event. As the sole designer, I created the brand, built the site, and produced the videos and social campaign in three months.

01

Building the brand

A logo, color palette, and typography system built to flex across five distinct product lines while staying recognizably one brand.

That foundation grew into the Brilliancy Standard, the master brand rulebook I own, defining how Brilliancy Quantum, Health, and Deep Tech look, sound, and present themselves, from business cards and decks to websites and swag.

Brilliancy Health website

Event branding

Event branding and web design for the division’s inaugural summit, bringing together healthcare executives, clinicians, and policymakers: hero sections, speaker profiles, agendas, venue details, and the social campaign behind it.

The summit sold out, with large-scale sponsors on board.

Pitch deck
