Sunset Zoo: Wayfinding Map & Signage System

Redesigned map. New signage system. Built to work across an entire zoo.

CLIENTSunset Zoo
ROLEFreelance Designer
SERVICESEnvironmental Design, Wayfinding, Visual Systems
YEAR2024

OVERVIEW

Sunset Zoo needed a clearer visitor map and a complete signage system, directional and informational, that could work across the entire park. As a freelance designer, I created both, and the relationship grew into event and seasonal work.

01

Consistency at scale

A signage system lives across dozens of applications (small directional signs, large exhibit panels, and printed maps), and every one has to read clearly at a glance.

02

One typographic system

I defined headline, label, and descriptor roles, then scaled and repositioned them for each format, so an area sign like Australian Walkabout, a trail marker, and a large exhibit panel all feel like the same family.

Bold silhouettes and a single accent color let each area keep its own personality while the system stays consistent.

03

The visitor map

The redesigned map carries the same visual language and makes the park easier to navigate.

It’s still handed to every guest at the entrance.

04

Exhibit signs

Four large exhibit signs for the tigers, leopards, and sloth bears: life-size animal cutouts on a warm wood-fence backdrop, so guests can stand right next to them.

05

Education that talks back

Each panel pairs one big headline fact with bite-size adaptation callouts, plus questions that get kids looking and comparing: “Are you taller than a sloth bear?” “Can you find a spot that looks like a smiley face?” QR codes lead to more.

06

Beyond wayfinding

The relationship expanded into event graphics and seasonal programming, including Earth Day campaigns and Brew at the Zoo branding.
